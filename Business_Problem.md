# Warehouse Inventory Slotting Optimization

## 1. Business Problem

E-commerce warehouses store thousands of products across
different locations. Inefficient product placement can cause
warehouse workers to travel longer distances while picking
customer orders.

Products that are frequently ordered together may be stored
far apart, increasing picking distance and potentially increasing
order-processing time.

## 2. Problem Statement

Develop a data-driven warehouse slotting optimization system
that analyzes historical order patterns, product demand,
product relationships, and warehouse locations to recommend
better product placements.

## 3. Objective

The objective is to identify product placement strategies
that can reduce estimated picking distance and improve
warehouse operational efficiency.

## 4. Proposed Solution

The system will:

1. Analyze historical order data.
2. Measure product demand and order frequency.
3. Identify products frequently purchased together.
4. Group products based on purchasing behavior.
5. Model warehouse locations and distances.
6. Recommend suitable product placements.
7. Compare current and recommended layouts.

## 5. Expected Business Benefits

- Reduce estimated picking distance.
- Reduce unnecessary worker movement.
- Improve order-picking efficiency.
- Support data-driven warehouse layout decisions.

## 6. SKU

SKU = Stock Keeping Unit

It is a unique identifier for a product.

Example:

SKU001 → Dell Laptop
SKU002 → Wireless Mouse
SKU003 → Mechanical Keyboard
