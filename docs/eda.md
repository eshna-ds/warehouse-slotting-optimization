## Objective

Analyze product demand and order behavior for warehouse slotting.

## Analysis

Order frequency: Identified frequently ordered products such as 85123A, 85099B, 22423.
Total quantity: Identified high-volume products such as 23843, 23166, 22197.
Products per order: Median = 15, Mean = 26.03.
Key Insight

Products with high order frequency are not always the same as products with high quantity.

Therefore, our slotting system should consider:

Order Frequency
       +
Total Quantity
       +
Product Affinity
       ↓
Better Warehouse Slot

## Outcome

EDA identified important products and established the need for product affinity analysis.
