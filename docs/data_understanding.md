## Dataset Summary

The dataset contains 541,909 transaction records
across 8 columns.

It contains:
- 25,900 unique invoices/orders
- 4,070 unique products (SKUs)
- 4,372 unique customers

The dataset provides transaction-level information,
which is useful for analyzing product demand and
identifying products that are frequently purchased
together.

The most frequently occurring StockCodes include
85123A, 22423, 85099B, 47566 and 20725.
