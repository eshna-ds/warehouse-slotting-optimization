## Objective

Evaluate whether the warehouse slot optimization actually reduces picking distance.

## Work Completed

Loaded order, current-location, and optimized-slot data.
Calculated the current picking distance for each order.
Calculated the optimized picking distance for each order.
Compared the before and after distances.

## Calculated:
Average distance before optimization
Average distance after optimization
Average distance saved
Improvement percentage
Number of products relocated

## Formula

Distance Saved =
Before Distance − After Distance
Improvement % =
(Before − After) / Before × 100

## Output Files

before_after_evaluation.csv
optimization_summary.csv

## Conclusion

Day 17 evaluates the actual impact of the proposed warehouse slotting optimization by comparing picking distance before and after relocation. The final improvement percentage is based on the calculated results from the project and should not be assumed or manually entered.
