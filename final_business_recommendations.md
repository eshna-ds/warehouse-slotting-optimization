## Objective

To convert the warehouse slotting results into actionable business recommendations.


## Tasks Completed

Analyzed final product clusters.
Identified high-priority products using picking priority.
Assigned recommended warehouse zones.
Generated product-level business recommendations.
Created the final recommendation table.

## Business Insight

High-volume products receive Zone A recommendations because they have high picking activity. Placing frequently handled products closer to the packing area can help reduce unnecessary picking travel.

## Final Business Flow

Product Data
     ↓
Product Features
     ↓
Product Clustering
     ↓
Product Movement Analysis
     ↓
Picking Priority
     ↓
Zone Recommendation
     ↓
Business Recommendation

## Conclusion

The project successfully converts historical order data into data-driven warehouse slotting 
recommendations, helping businesses decide which products should receive more accessible 
warehouse locations.
